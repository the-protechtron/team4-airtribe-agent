# Common Fever / Viral Illness

## Common symptoms
- Raised body temperature, chills, or sweating
- Body aches and headache
- Sore throat, runny or blocked nose
- Mild cough
- Tiredness or reduced appetite

## Ask the patient about
- When did the fever start and how high does it feel (touch, sweating, shivering)?
- Is the fever constant or does it come and go in a pattern?
- Any cough, sore throat, rash, joint pain, or vomiting alongside the fever?
- Has the patient traveled recently or been near anyone else who is sick?
- Any medicines already taken for the fever, and did they help?
- Are there ongoing illnesses (diabetes, heart disease) or is the patient pregnant, very young, or elderly?

## Red flags — seek urgent/emergency care immediately
- Fever lasting more than 3-5 days without improvement
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or severe weakness
- Stiff neck, confusion, seizures, or unresponsiveness
- Persistent vomiting, inability to keep fluids down
- Spreading rash, bleeding gums, or bruising
- Fever in a newborn, infant under 3 months, or pregnant woman

## Plain-language guidance
Rest, drink plenty of fluids, and eat light meals as tolerated; keep the person cool and comfortable while monitoring the fever's pattern and any new symptoms. If the fever persists beyond a few days, is very high, or any red-flag symptom appears, travel to a clinic promptly — a doctor should review the full picture before any treatment decision is made.
