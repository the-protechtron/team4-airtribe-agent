# Pregnancy Warning Signs / Maternal Health Red Flags

## Common symptoms (normal pregnancy discomforts, for context)
- Mild nausea, especially in early pregnancy
- Fatigue
- Mild swelling of feet, especially later in the day
- Back discomfort
- Occasional Braxton Hicks (practice) contractions later in pregnancy

## Ask the patient about
- How many weeks pregnant is the patient, and has she had regular prenatal checkups?
- Any bleeding, unusual discharge, or leaking fluid from the vagina?
- Any severe headache, blurred vision, or swelling of the face/hands?
- Is the baby's movement normal, reduced, or absent (if far enough along)?
- Any fever, severe abdominal pain, or contractions before the expected due date?
- Any prior pregnancy complications or existing conditions like high blood pressure or diabetes?

## Red flags — seek urgent/emergency care immediately
- Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy
- Severe headache, blurred vision, or swelling of face/hands (possible pre-eclampsia)
- Severe abdominal pain
- Reduced or absent baby movement
- Fever, chills, or foul-smelling discharge
- Fluid leaking before labor, or contractions well before the due date
- Seizures or loss of consciousness

## Plain-language guidance
Regular prenatal checkups matter throughout pregnancy, and any bleeding, severe headache, vision changes, swelling, reduced baby movement, or severe pain should be treated as urgent and evaluated at a clinic or hospital without delay. Encourage rest, hydration, and continued prenatal care between visits. A doctor or midwife should review the full picture, as maternal warning signs can change quickly and need prompt attention.
