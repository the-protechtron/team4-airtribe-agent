# Anemia

## Common symptoms
- Persistent tiredness and weakness
- Pale skin, lips, or inner eyelids
- Shortness of breath with mild activity
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Rapid heartbeat
- Brittle nails or hair loss (in longer-standing cases)

## Ask the patient about
- How long has the tiredness or weakness been present?
- Any noticeable paleness of skin, nails, or inside the eyelids?
- Any heavy menstrual bleeding, blood loss, or dark/tarry stools?
- Diet habits — is iron-rich food regularly eaten?
- Is the person pregnant, a young child, or recovering from illness?
- Any shortness of breath or rapid heartbeat with normal activity?

## Red flags — seek urgent/emergency care immediately
- Severe shortness of breath or chest pain
- Fainting or near-fainting episodes
- Rapid heartbeat with dizziness
- Visible blood loss (heavy bleeding, blood in stool/vomit)
- Extreme pallor with confusion or weakness
- Anemia symptoms in a pregnant woman or young child, which need prompt evaluation

## Plain-language guidance
Ongoing tiredness, paleness, and breathlessness with normal activity may point to low blood levels (anemia) and should be checked with a simple blood test at a clinic, along with looking for possible causes like poor diet or blood loss. Encourage iron-rich foods such as leafy greens and legumes in the meantime, but avoid self-starting any supplements without guidance. A doctor should review test results and the full picture before recommending treatment.
