# Diabetes Warning Signs

## Common symptoms
- Excessive thirst and frequent urination
- Unexplained weight loss
- Constant hunger
- Fatigue and weakness
- Blurred vision
- Slow-healing wounds or frequent infections

## Ask the patient about
- How long have symptoms like excess thirst or frequent urination been present?
- Any unexplained weight loss despite normal or increased eating?
- Any wounds, cuts, or infections that are healing slowly?
- Family history of diabetes?
- Any tingling, numbness, or vision changes noticed?
- Is the person diagnosed already and on any regular treatment?

## Red flags — seek urgent/emergency care immediately
- Confusion, extreme drowsiness, or difficulty waking up
- Rapid breathing with a fruity-smelling breath
- Severe dehydration, persistent vomiting, or abdominal pain
- Very high or very low measured blood sugar with symptoms
- Sudden vision loss
- Non-healing wound with spreading redness, swelling, or fever

## Plain-language guidance
Persistent excess thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss, or slow-healing wounds should prompt a blood sugar check at a clinic, since diabetes managed early avoids serious complications. Encourage regular meals, avoiding prolonged fasting, and monitoring for the red-flag signs above, which can indicate a dangerous blood sugar emergency. A doctor should review test results and the full picture before starting or adjusting any treatment.
